## CEFR Level: A1 — Beginner | Skill: Writing

The learner is a complete beginner. The goal is to practise **writing Dutch** — correct spelling, letter-by-letter dictation, and simple written forms (names, addresses, dates, short messages).

### Language Complexity Constraints (A1)
- Maximum 8 words per dialogue line
- Present tense only
- Use only the most common 500 Dutch words
- Introduce at most 8 new vocabulary items per episode
- Repeat each key phrase at least 3 times
- No subordinate clauses
- Numbers: 1–20 only
- No idioms

### Skill Focus: Writing

The viewer is **writing Dutch alongside the video** — spelling words out, filling gaps, and composing very short messages. The dialogue should:

- Include **dictation sequences**: SpeakerA says a word or short phrase, then spells it out letter by letter so the viewer can write it down
  - Format: `"Schrijf dit op: [word]. [letter]-[letter]-[letter]. Dat is [word]."`
- Simulate a real writing task (filling in a form, writing a shopping list, composing a short SMS)
- Pause for the viewer to write: use `<break time="3s"/>` lines where SpeakerA says "Schrijf het op!" or "Jouw beurt!"
- Explicitly teach the Dutch alphabet letter names for tricky letters (G, IJ, Y, UI)
- In the Recap phase, repeat key words with spelling drills — SpeakerA spells, SpeakerB confirms
- Quiz questions test spelling and written form: "Hoe schrijf je dit woord?" / fill-in-the-blank
- Prefer `fill_gap` quiz type (dominant); also `multiple_choice` for spelling variants

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~15 | Introduce the writing task (e.g. "Vandaag schrijven we een boodschappenlijst"), prepare viewer to write |
| Core Dialogue | ~65 | Walk through the writing task step by step with dictation and spelling drills |
| Phrase Recap | ~46 | Spelling recap — SpeakerA spells each key word, SpeakerB confirms; writing tips |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; questions ask viewer to recall spelling or complete a phrase |

**Total target: ~179 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA is a writing coach; SpeakerB models the writing process (asks "Hoe schrijf je dat?" etc.)
- After each dictation item, SpeakerA confirms: "Goed zo! [word] schrijf je met een [letter]."
- Include a tip about a common Dutch spelling rule (e.g. long vs. short vowels, de/het distinction)
- Start the introduction by telling the viewer to get a pen and paper ready
