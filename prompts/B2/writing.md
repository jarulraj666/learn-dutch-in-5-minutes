## CEFR Level: B2 — Upper Intermediate | Skill: Writing

The learner is upper intermediate. The goal is to write clear, well-structured texts on complex topics — argumentative essays, formal letters, reports, and summaries — with varied vocabulary and accurate grammar.

### Language Complexity Constraints (B2)
- Up to 25 words per dialogue line; complex multi-clause sentences
- All tenses; passive voice; nominalisations; subjunctive mood where applicable
- Wide vocabulary; introduce at most 20 new items per episode; formal register expected
- Logical and rhetorical connectors: "bovendien", "desondanks", "daarentegen", "met andere woorden", "kortom"
- Dutch academic and journalistic writing conventions

### Skill Focus: Writing

The viewer practises writing a complex, well-argued Dutch text with appropriate register and cohesion. The dialogue should:

- Simulate a B2 writing task: an argumentative essay paragraph, a formal complaint letter, a business proposal introduction, a critical summary
- Teach paragraph cohesion: how to link sentences with varied connectors and pronoun reference
- SpeakerA dictates a complex sentence; SpeakerB writes and reads back — SpeakerA corrects and explains
- Focus on common B2 writing errors: incorrect subordinate clause word order, wrong passive construction, de/het with abstract nouns
- Style coaching: avoid repetition ("zei" → "beweerde", "stelde", "merkte op"), vary sentence length for rhythm
- In the Recap phase, SpeakerA reads the complete polished text; discusses 3 key style choices made
- Quiz: choose correct connector, transform a sentence to passive, or select the more formal synonym
- Prefer `fill_gap` (dominant) and `multiple_choice` quiz types

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~8 | Introduce writing task, audience, and purpose; discuss register and tone |
| Core Dialogue | ~90 | Complex dictation; cohesion; passive; formal vocabulary; style improvements |
| Phrase Recap | ~40 | Read complete polished text; discuss style choices; highlight B2 writing features |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; connectors, passives, formal synonyms, verb forms |

**Total target: ~191 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA is a writing coach who comments on both accuracy and style
- SpeakerB writes a "first draft" version with typical B2 errors; SpeakerA models the improved version
- Include a grammar note on the Dutch passive voice (worden + past participle) and when to use it vs. active
