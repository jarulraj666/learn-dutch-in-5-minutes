## CEFR Level: B2 — Upper Intermediate | Skill: Reading

The learner is upper intermediate. The goal is to read and understand complex texts — opinion articles, academic introductions, professional reports, and literary prose — extracting both explicit and implicit meaning.

### Language Complexity Constraints (B2)
- Up to 25 words per dialogue line; complex sentence structures
- All tenses; passive voice; nominalisations ("het gebruik van...", "de invoering van...")
- Wide vocabulary; introduce at most 20 new items per episode; include collocations and formal vocabulary
- Embedded clauses, concessive and conditional structures
- Formal register; Dutch text conventions for journalism and academic writing

### Skill Focus: Reading

The viewer reads and critically analyses a B2-level Dutch text. The dialogue should:

- Use a real B2 text type: an opinion column (column/opiniestuk), an academic abstract, a formal report introduction, or a literary passage
- SpeakerA reads the text in meaningful segments; SpeakerB analyses and interprets
- Teach critical reading: identify the author's argument, detect bias, distinguish fact from opinion
- Vocabulary focus: nominalisations, formal synonyms ("gebruik" → "aanwending"), false cognates
- Discuss text structure: introduction, thesis statement, argumentation, conclusion in Dutch journalism/academic writing
- In the Recap phase, SpeakerB gives a critical summary including their own view on the text
- Quiz: critical comprehension, inference, vocabulary-in-context, author's intent
- Prefer `multiple_choice`; include inference and author's-purpose questions

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~8 | Present the text source and type; discuss the topic; set a critical reading question |
| Core Dialogue | ~90 | Segment-by-segment reading and critical analysis; formal vocabulary; argument identification |
| Phrase Recap | ~40 | SpeakerB's critical summary; key formal phrases highlighted |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; critical comprehension and vocabulary-in-context |

**Total target: ~191 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA models expert critical reading; SpeakerB offers interpretations and is occasionally challenged
- Teach at least one text analysis technique: "Zoek de stelling van de auteur in de eerste twee zinnen"
- Include a grammar note on a B2 structure from the text (e.g. passive nominalisations, embedded relative clauses)
