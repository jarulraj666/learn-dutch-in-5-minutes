## CEFR Level: B2 — Upper Intermediate | Skill: Listening

The learner is upper intermediate. The goal is to understand extended speech on abstract, complex, or technical topics — debates, lectures, podcasts, and nuanced discussions.

### Language Complexity Constraints (B2)
- Up to 25 words per dialogue line; complex multi-clause sentences allowed
- All tenses including conditional (zou + infinitive) and past perfect (had gedaan)
- Wide vocabulary; introduce at most 20 new items per episode, including collocations and idioms
- Complex subordinate structures: embedded clauses, concessive clauses ("hoewel", "ondanks dat")
- Abstract nouns, nominalisations, formal register
- Dutch idioms and fixed expressions

### Skill Focus: Listening

The viewer trains to extract meaning from dense, fast, nuanced spoken Dutch. The dialogue should:

- Simulate authentic B2 listening scenarios: a radio debate, a TED-style talk excerpt, a business meeting, a podcast
- Include longer speaker turns (5–8 sentences); listener must track argument structure, not just facts
- Teach critical listening: distinguish fact vs. opinion, main argument vs. supporting detail
- Include pragmatic nuance: irony, hedging ("misschien wel", "het zou kunnen"), emphasis
- Comprehension strategies: predict from context, use discourse markers to track structure
- Quiz: critical comprehension — "Wat is het hoofdargument?" / "Hoe reageert SpeakerB op dit punt?" / inference
- Prefer `multiple_choice` and `listen_and_pick` quiz types; include at least 2 inference questions

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~8 | Frame the listening scenario; activate background knowledge; set the critical listening task |
| Core Dialogue | ~90 | Extended debate/discussion; complex ideas; hedging; discourse markers; argument tracking |
| Phrase Recap | ~40 | Summarise the debate positions; highlight key B2 discourse phrases |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; critical comprehension and inference |

**Total target: ~191 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA and SpeakerB hold a genuine intellectual debate with opposing viewpoints — they agree and disagree substantively
- Natural B2 speech features: ellipsis, topic fronting ("Die film, die heb ik niet gezien"), discourse particles
- Include a note on a pragmatic feature of spoken Dutch (hedging, irony, register shift)
