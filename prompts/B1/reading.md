## CEFR Level: B1 — Intermediate | Skill: Reading

The learner has solid basic Dutch. The goal is to read and understand the main points of texts on familiar subjects — articles, blog posts, instructions, and straightforward descriptions.

### Language Complexity Constraints (B1)
- Maximum 18 words per dialogue line
- All major tenses; passive voice introduced
- Common 2000-word vocabulary; introduce at most 16 new items per episode
- Subordinate and relative clauses
- Connectors for contrast, cause, and sequence: "hoewel", "terwijl", "daarna", "bovendien"

### Skill Focus: Reading

The viewer reads and comprehends a B1-level Dutch text, applying active reading strategies. The dialogue should:

- Use a real B1 text type: a short newspaper article (4–6 sentences), a blog post paragraph, an instruction manual section
- SpeakerA reads the text in chunks (2–3 sentences); SpeakerB summarises each chunk in their own words
- Teach skimming and scanning: "Zoek naar de kernzin in deze alinea" (find the topic sentence)
- Identify text structure: topic sentence, supporting details, conclusion
- Unfamiliar words: teach guessing from context and from word parts (prefixes: ont-, ver-, be-; suffixes: -heid, -ing)
- In the Recap phase, SpeakerB gives a full oral summary of the text without reading it back
- Quiz: inference, detail, and vocabulary-in-context questions
- Prefer `multiple_choice` quiz type; also `fill_gap` for vocabulary-in-context

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~10 | Present the text; activate background knowledge on the topic |
| Core Dialogue | ~80 | Chunk-by-chunk reading; comprehension questions; vocabulary strategies |
| Phrase Recap | ~44 | Oral summary by SpeakerB; highlight key phrases from text |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; comprehension and vocabulary questions |

**Total target: ~187 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA models expert reader behaviour (predicting, inferring, identifying structure)
- SpeakerB models the learner — tries, makes reasonable guesses, asks for clarification
- Include a grammar note on a structure from the text (e.g. passive voice, separable verbs, relative clauses)
