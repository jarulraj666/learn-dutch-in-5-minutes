## CEFR Level: B1 — Intermediate | Skill: Listening

The learner has solid basic Dutch. The goal is to understand the main points of clear speech on familiar topics — news, instructions, conversations about work, travel, and opinions.

### Language Complexity Constraints (B1)
- Maximum 18 words per dialogue line
- Present, past, and perfect tenses (ik heb gedaan, ik was gegaan)
- Common 2000-word vocabulary; introduce at most 16 new items per episode
- Subordinate clauses with: "omdat", "als", "wanneer", "hoewel", "zodat", "terwijl"
- Relative clauses: "die", "dat"
- Numbers: all; dates, times, percentages
- Fixed expressions, collocations, and common idioms

### Skill Focus: Listening

The viewer trains to follow B1-level spoken Dutch — more natural pace, longer sentences, opinion expressions, and logical connectors. The dialogue should:

- Simulate realistic listening scenarios: a short radio news item, a colleague explaining a plan, a phone call
- Include longer turns (3–4 sentences) where the listener must track the main point, not just individual words
- Teach note-taking strategies: "Luister naar het hoofdidee" (listen for the main idea)
- Use contrast and opinion markers: "aan de ene kant... aan de andere kant", "ik vind dat..."
- In the Recap phase, SpeakerA summarises a section in 2 sentences; SpeakerB adds a detail
- Quiz: inference and main-idea questions: "Wat is de mening van SpeakerB?" / "Wat is het probleem?"
- Prefer `multiple_choice` and `listen_and_pick` quiz types

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~10 | Introduce the listening scenario; briefly frame topic context |
| Core Dialogue | ~80 | B1 conversation with longer turns; opinion expressions; logical connectors |
| Phrase Recap | ~44 | Summarise main points; SpeakerB adds details; paraphrase key statements |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; inference and main-idea questions |

**Total target: ~187 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerB carries longer turns (stories, opinions, explanations); SpeakerA listens actively and asks clarifying questions
- Natural speech features: discourse markers ("nou", "eigenlijk", "trouwens", "weet je"), mild contractions
- Occasionally SpeakerA asks: "Wacht even, wat bedoel je precies met...?" to model active listening
