## CEFR Level: B1 — Intermediate | Skill: Writing

The learner has solid basic Dutch. The goal is to write connected texts on familiar topics — structured paragraphs, a short essay, a formal email, or a personal letter.

### Language Complexity Constraints (B1)
- Maximum 18 words per dialogue line
- All major tenses; passive voice
- Common 2000-word vocabulary; introduce at most 16 new items per episode
- Subordinate and relative clauses
- Logical connectors: "bovendien", "echter", "daarom", "aan de andere kant", "ten slotte"

### Skill Focus: Writing

The viewer practises writing structured, coherent Dutch texts with correct grammar and appropriate register. The dialogue should:

- Simulate a real B1 writing task: a formal complaint email, a short opinion paragraph, a letter to a landlord
- Teach paragraph structure: topic sentence → supporting sentences → concluding sentence
- Dictate the text in chunks; SpeakerB reads back and SpeakerA gives corrective feedback
- Teach formal vs. informal register: "u" vs "jij", formal sign-offs ("Met vriendelijke groet" vs "Groetjes")
- Common B1 writing pitfalls: word order in subordinate clauses, de/het with abstract nouns, separable verbs in writing
- In the Recap phase, SpeakerA reads the complete corrected text; highlights 3 key writing improvements
- Quiz: choose the correct connector or verb form to complete a sentence in the text
- Prefer `fill_gap` (dominant) and `multiple_choice` quiz types

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~10 | Introduce writing task and audience; discuss register choice |
| Core Dialogue | ~80 | Structured dictation; paragraph building; grammar correction feedback |
| Phrase Recap | ~44 | Read complete text back; highlight key structures and improvements |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; fill connectors, verb forms, register choices |

**Total target: ~187 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA is the writing coach; SpeakerB writes and reads back — makes typical B1 errors for SpeakerA to correct
- After corrections, explain the rule briefly: "In een bijzin staat het werkwoord aan het einde, dus..."
- Include a grammar note on subordinate clause word order (the most impactful B1 writing skill)
