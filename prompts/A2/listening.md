## CEFR Level: A2 — Elementary | Skill: Listening

The learner has basic Dutch. The goal is to train comprehension of slightly more varied spoken Dutch — longer sentences, simple past tense, and more connected speech.

### Language Complexity Constraints (A2)
- Maximum 14 words per dialogue line
- Present simple + simple past tense (ik was, ik ging, ik had)
- Use common 1000-word vocabulary
- Introduce at most 12 new vocabulary items per episode
- Repeat each key phrase at least 2 times
- Simple subordinate clauses allowed: "omdat", "maar", "en", "want"
- Numbers: 1–100
- Common fixed expressions and polite phrases (alsjeblieft, bedankt, sorry)

### Skill Focus: Listening

The viewer trains to understand naturally flowing A2-level Dutch speech. The dialogue should:

- Include slightly longer utterances than A1 — sentences can be 2 short clauses joined with "en", "maar", or "omdat"
- Simulate real A2 listening scenarios: short announcements, a friend telling a story, following simple instructions
- SpeakerA occasionally summarises what was just said: "Dus, SpeakerB zegt dat..."
- Comprehension checks: "Heb je dat begrepen?" or "Wat denk je dat hij bedoelde?"
- In the Recap phase, replay key utterances — SpeakerA says the phrase, SpeakerB paraphrases it in simpler words
- Quiz questions test detailed comprehension: "Wat deed SpeakerB gisteren?" / "Welke dag noemde SpeakerA?"
- Prefer `listen_and_pick` and `multiple_choice` quiz types

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~12 | Greet, introduce topic and skill, briefly recap A1 if needed |
| Core Dialogue | ~70 | A2-level conversation; mix of present and past tense; comprehension pauses |
| Phrase Recap | ~46 | Replay key phrases with brief paraphrasing; comprehension-focused drills |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; test nuanced listening comprehension |

**Total target: ~181 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerB tells short anecdotes (past tense): "Gisteren ging ik naar..." — SpeakerA reacts and asks follow-up questions
- Slightly faster pace than A1 but still deliberate; occasional natural speech features (elisions, contractions)
- Every 10–12 lines, SpeakerA pauses to check: "Begrijp je wat SpeakerB bedoelde?"
