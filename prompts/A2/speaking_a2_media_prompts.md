# A2 Speaking Media Prompts - Exam 1

Use these prompts to produce functional media for an original A2 speaking mock exam. They follow the four-part DUO-inspired pattern but are not copies of DUO assessment content. Do not add text, logos, labels, subtitles, speech bubbles or watermarks to any media.
