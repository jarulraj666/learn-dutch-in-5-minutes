## CEFR Level: A2 — Elementary | Skill: Writing

The learner has basic Dutch. The goal is to write short connected texts — a simple email, a short message to a friend, filling in a form with a few sentences, or a brief description of themselves.

### Language Complexity Constraints (A2)
- Maximum 14 words per dialogue line
- Present simple + simple past tense
- Common 1000-word vocabulary
- Introduce at most 12 new vocabulary items per episode
- Simple connectors: "en", "maar", "want", "ook", "dan"
- Numbers: 1–100

### Skill Focus: Writing

The viewer practises composing short A2-level Dutch texts with correct grammar and spelling. The dialogue should:

- Simulate a real writing task: a short email ("Beste..., ...Met vriendelijke groet"), a postcard, a short bio
- SpeakerA dictates one sentence at a time; SpeakerB writes along and reads back what was written
- Teach standard Dutch text conventions: capitalisation, salutations, sign-offs for emails
- Include 1–2 common Dutch abbreviations and their full forms (e.g. a.u.b. = alstublieft, t/m = tot en met)
- Spelling focus: de/het article choice, -d vs -dt (loop**t** vs. loop**d**), common double letters
- In the Recap phase, SpeakerA reads back the completed text; SpeakerB corrects any "mistakes" (model correction)
- Quiz: fill-in-the-blank sentences within the written text; correct article (de/het); correct verb form
- Prefer `fill_gap` (dominant) and `multiple_choice` quiz types

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Lines | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Introduction | ~12 | Introduce the writing task and its real-world purpose; tell viewer to open a notebook |
| Core Dialogue | ~70 | Dictation of the text; spelling rules; punctuation; de/het; verb forms |
| Phrase Recap | ~46 | Read back the complete text; correct errors; highlight key spelling rules |
| Quiz | ~53 | 5-line announcement + 8 × 6-line rounds; fill gaps in the text or choose correct form |

**Total target: ~181 dialogue lines**

### Dialogue Style
- SpeakerA is the writing teacher; SpeakerB is the writer who makes occasional deliberate A2 mistakes so SpeakerA can correct them
- After each correction, briefly explain the rule: "Je schrijft 'loopt' met een t, omdat de stam 'loop' is en..."
- Include a grammar note on the Dutch -d/-dt/-t verb rule (very common source of writing errors)
